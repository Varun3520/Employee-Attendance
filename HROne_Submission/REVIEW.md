# REVIEW.md

The following issues were found in the starter implementation and addressed in the revised `app/main.py`.

| # | Where | What was wrong | How to notice | How it was fixed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `compute_late_minutes` | It compared whole minutes to `10`, so `09:40:01` incorrectly became 0 late minutes; it also reused the punch-in calendar date and ignored the attendance date for overnight shifts. | For a `09:30` shift, test `09:40:00`, `09:40:01`, and `10:15:59`; expected 0, 10, and 45. Test an overnight shift. | The helper compares instants against the shift start on the attendance date and applies the strict 600-second grace condition before flooring elapsed minutes. |
| 2 | `compute_work_hours` | Python's built-in `round` uses ties-to-even and does not guarantee the contract's half-up rule. | Use a duration whose hours round at a half-cent boundary. | The helper uses `Decimal` and `ROUND_HALF_UP` to two decimals. |
| 3 | `compute_overtime` | It built a local IST shift-end datetime and could compare it incorrectly with UTC/naive values; it did not correctly advance the shift end for overnight shifts or enforce the 30-minute minimum. | Test a 5-minute overtime period, a 40-minute period, and a 22:00-06:00 shift. | Shift end is constructed in IST on the attendance date, advanced one day when overnight, converted to UTC, and counted only at 30 minutes or more. |
| 4 | Startup / indexes | No startup lifecycle created the unique and query-supporting indexes required by the contract. Check-then-insert alone cannot protect against concurrent requests. | Start with a database containing only `_id` indexes and inspect the created indexes; send concurrent duplicate punch-ins. | Idempotent indexes are created at startup, including unique employee and employee/date keys plus indexes for list and analytics queries. |
| 5 | `GET /health` | It returned success without checking MongoDB readiness. | Stop/unavailable MongoDB and call `/health`. | It pings MongoDB and returns 503 on a PyMongo failure. |
| 6 | `POST /employees` | It used a race-prone duplicate pre-check, stored a naive creation timestamp, returned a datetime instead of epoch milliseconds, and had no contract-level input validation. | Send duplicate requests concurrently; try malformed employee codes, dates, emails, shift times, equal shifts, and length violations. | The unique index enforces conflicts; Pydantic validates the input; `created_at` is UTC in storage and epoch milliseconds in the response. |
| 7 | `GET /employees` | Pagination skipped `page * page_size` rather than `(page - 1) * page_size`; total counted the unfiltered collection; ordering and range validation were missing. | Test page 1 with size 2, a department filter, and invalid page values. | Uses a filtered count, deterministic `emp_code` sort, bounded query parameters and correct skip/limit; converts `created_at`. |
| 8 | `POST /attendance/punch-in` | It used the server's local timezone, a truthiness check for `punched_at`, no employee/status/timestamp validation, a check-then-insert race, incorrect overnight date logic and leaked a record id. | Test unknown employee, seconds/floats/strings, 09:40:01, overnight shifts, and parallel duplicate requests. | Uses strict epoch milliseconds, UTC/IST conversions, the unique natural-key index and the contract response fields without `_id`. |
| 9 | `POST /attendance/punch-out` | The endpoint was missing. | Call the contract route or test work-hour, overtime and half-day rules. | Implemented with 24-hour validation, half-up work-hour rounding, overnight shift handling and a conditional update so simultaneous punch-outs cannot both succeed. |
| 10 | `GET /attendance` | It read the entire collection into Python memory, sorted and paginated in Python, exposed internal ids, returned raw BSON datetimes, and did not validate date filters or pagination. | Test on a large dataset and inspect records containing history timestamps. | It uses indexed MongoDB filters, sort, skip and limit, validates the filters, and serializes all times to epoch milliseconds. |
| 11 | `PATCH /attendance/{emp_code}/{date}` | The endpoint was missing; the contract also requires recomputing derived fields, exact change-only history, and safe concurrent corrections. | Test LEAVE/PRESENT transitions, invalid punch dates, >24-hour durations, unchanged requests and parallel corrections. | Implemented regularization with time/date/status validation, derived-field recomputation, a history append, and optimistic conditional updates returning 409 on stale concurrent edits. |
| 12 | Employee monthly analytics | The endpoint was missing; its required aggregation must handle employees with no logs and mid-month joiners. | Test an employee with no logs and a mid-month `joined_on`. | Uses an employee-rooted aggregation with a date calendar and indexed lookup of monthly logs. |
| 13 | Department summary | The endpoint was missing; a summary rooted only in attendance would lose employees with no logs and average of employee averages would be wrong. | Include no-log employees, a future joiner, weekend records and null work hours. | Uses an employee-rooted cross-collection aggregation, preserves employees with no logs, deduplicates headcount, limits present days to weekdays and averages eligible records. |
| 14 | Late leaderboard | The endpoint was missing; array-position ranking and limiting before ranking omit tied rows at the cutoff. | Test ties at ranks 1/2 or at the requested limit. | Uses `$setWindowFields` with `$rank`, filters `rank <= limit` after ranking and orders tied rows by `emp_code`. |
| 15 | Department trend | The endpoint was missing; Python-side gap filling would violate the database-side requirement and could omit weekends or empty days. | Use a range with no logs, weekends and newly joined employees. | An aggregation creates every day, computes dynamic headcount and counts, and uses a seven-document window for the non-null moving average. |
| 16 | Explain endpoint | The endpoint was missing; fixed representative pipelines cannot demonstrate the actual endpoint's filters and pagination. | Change endpoint parameters and compare the explained pipeline. | The endpoint calls the same pipeline builders and applies the same list query, sort, skip and limit, with `executionStats` verbosity. |

## Reviewed and retained

- The natural attendance key is `(emp_code, date)`, so a unique compound index is required and appropriate.
- `history` remains manual-correction-only; punch-in and punch-out do not append history entries.
- Analytics read the stored `late_minutes`, `overtime_minutes`, `work_hours` and `half_day`; they do not recalculate those stored values from timestamps.
- Calendar fields remain `YYYY-MM-DD` / `YYYY-MM` strings. Instants are BSON UTC datetimes in MongoDB and epoch milliseconds in responses.
